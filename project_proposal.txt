Problem Statement: Address the difficulties in predicting post-perturbation gene expression profiles given a primary (control) gene expression state, context (chemical perturbation cell type, etc), specifically those related to cell-line confounding and experimental variation. 
Objectives:
1) Train an adversarial deconfounding autoencoder (ADAE; improved generalizability/performance in noisy contexts) model to predict gene expression profiles given a drug perturbation,context, and baseline state.
2) Improve/evaluate generalizability to held-out drugs/cell lines.
3) Evaluate biological relevancy with gene and pathway level metrics
4) Test whether learned embeddings group drugs by biological function. 

Drug perturbation (and RNASeq in general; single cell/bulk/otherwise) are notoriously noisy data, and the feature space is highly interactive (gene-gene interactions). Deep learning is thusly appropriate to address this problem.

Datasets: Tahoe100 (training; initial validation) and SciPlex (secondary validation)
